Rahul Subbaraman

Life is too interesting to have just one passion. This site is a digital archive where I collect memories, stories, ideas, perspectives and professional milestones. By training and trade, I am a Quantitative Earth Scientist, currently an Interim Associate Editor for Communications Earth & Environment at Springer Nature Technology and Publishing Solutions. I have completed a PhD in Earth Sciences from the University of Manchester specialising in volcanic petrology.
